# Evaluation: Cybersecurity – Third-Party Risk Management Report

## Overall Assessment

Structurally strong and relevant, but the output has visible polish and source-quality issues. The report covers an important enterprise topic and includes useful executive themes, but it should not be the lead demo artifact without review. It is valuable as an evaluation sample because it exposes realistic prototype limitations.

## Strengths

- Good topic relevance for Fortune 500 buyers.
- Clear pain points around vendor risk, supply chain exposure, and continuous monitoring.
- Useful strategic opportunity framing.
- Demonstrates that the system can handle cybersecurity topics.

## Gaps / Limitations

- Source quality is uneven.
- One source title appears malformed/ugly in the generated output.
- VC investment analysis is too light.
- Cybersecurity production use should rely on stronger sources: analyst reports, regulatory guidance, breach databases, vendor-risk benchmarks, and customer case studies.

## Recommended Demo Positioning

Do not lead with this report. Use it internally as a stress-test output and discuss in the evaluation notes as evidence that source scoring, citation validation, and formatting review are important production next steps.
